Crawl Space Water Removal in Roswell

Few homeowners notice right away when water collects beneath the floor. It tends to turn up as a damp chill underfoot, a stale smell at the vents, or a small lake discovered the day somebody lifts the hatch. By then the insulation is usually soaked and the wood above it has been wet for days. Our team pumps the water out, clears what is ruined and dries the framing before it softens.

Pumping is just half of the work. Because lumber under a floor is slow to soak and slow to dry, our crew tracks its moisture content with a meter and runs dehumidification for the 3 to 5 days it normally takes to bring the readings down. Finding the cause matters just as much, since a dripping water line, a failed drain or groundwater from a storm will flood the area again unless it is addressed.

Warning Signs

  • Flooring overhead that seems chilly, clammy, spongy or springy underfoot
  • Moisture loving pests such as camel crickets and silverfish appearing indoors
  • A musty, earthy smell that is strongest near the floor vents
  • Insulation that has pulled loose and hangs in wet clumps below the joists
  • A look through the hatch shows pooled water or soupy soil
  • Tide lines or white mineral crust on the piers and foundation walls
How We Fix It

How We Handle Crawl Space Water Removal

1

Safety Check and Inspection

We check for electrical hazards, sagging ducts and contaminated water before anyone crawls in. After that our team marks out the wet area and records how much moisture the framing and subfloor hold.

2

Pump Out Water

Standing water comes out with submersible pumps and hoses, down to the footing trenches and dips where it gathers. Mud and silt left on the liner are cleared at the same time.

3

Remove Wet Materials

Debris, saturated batts and any liner that cannot be reused leave the crawl space in bags. Exposed joists and subfloor are cleaned and treated with an antimicrobial where growth has started or is likely.

4

Final Drying and Checks

Compact fans and dehumidification work in the crawl space as long as it takes for the framing to test normal. A final meter check follows, and you receive the numbers along with steps to prevent a repeat.

Behind the Surface

Risks That Are Easy to Miss

Air From Below

Because air naturally moves upward from under the home, spores and stale odors in a wet crawl space reach your living areas too.

Termites and Other Pests

A wet crawl space offers the soft wood and moisture that wood destroying insects and mice prefer. Getting moisture levels down takes away a main reason for them to stay.

Settling Under the House

Saturated soil can soften around footings and let piers settle or lean. You might notice it as a door that suddenly rubs or a floor that is no longer level.

Contaminated Water

What collects beneath a home is frequently dirty. If it came from a drain pipe or from waste laden groundwater it is rated Category 2 or 3, so we suit up and disinfect.

How the Damage Progresses

First day

Water Pools Below

Water collects in the low spots and soaks into the soil. Humidity in the space jumps within hours as the insulation pulls water into itself.

Hours 24 to 48

Early Mold Growth

Mold can start on damp joists and paper faced insulation. Wet batts get heavy and begin to pull away from the subfloor.

Days 3 through 7

Moisture Moves Upstairs

Damp air rises into the living space. You might see floorboards cupping and notice a musty odor while the framing continues to soak.

After a week

Wood Starts Rotting

Lumber left damp this long starts breaking down. Joists and sill plates soften, fasteners rust and the damp space attracts termites and other pests.
